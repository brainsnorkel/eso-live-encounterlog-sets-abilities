### Added
- **Your fight history** (#170): every boss fight is kept across sessions, kill or not, in `fights.jsonl` beside the settings, so a pull reads against your group's earlier kills of the boss: `Your Lord Falgravn kills: median 441k over 28, best 953k; with 8+ of this roster on 12.1: median 470k over 10. This pull: 512k` under the fight's header. A player's name hover adds their boss DPS in their last three kills of the boss, and a boss's pulls in a row end with the best earlier kill with this roster. **Settings → Fight history → Build from split logs** reads your split logs into it in the background, and carries on where it stopped after a Cancel. **Hide names** hides the handles in all of it
