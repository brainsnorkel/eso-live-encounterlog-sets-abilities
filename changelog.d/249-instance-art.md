### Added
- **A trial's or an arena's art on its header and its page** (#249): in the fight list, the header of a group fought in a trial or an arena is a little taller and carries a strip of the game's loading-screen painting for that instance after its name, and the same painting runs across the top of the Instance tab. All fourteen trials and the five arenas have one; a dungeon, the open world, and a trial newer than the app keep the plain header. The art sits beside the name, never under the fights' text, says nothing to a screen reader, and is off while the system asks for high contrast. Settings → Display → *Instance art* turns it off (on for a new install)
