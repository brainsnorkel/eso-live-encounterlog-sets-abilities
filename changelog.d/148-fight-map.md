### Added
- **A map of where the group stood** (#148): right of the fight's header, the game's own map, cropped to the fight's area, with a blue heat of where the group spent the fight and its hottest area outlined. A fight that crossed maps (a Sunspire trash pull into the Temple Grounds) has a small map for each, side by side. Click one for a larger map window, with a tab per map and a switch to see the whole map. Maps of trials, dungeons, arenas, the Infinite Archive, solo dungeons, the Night Market and the Elinhir Private Arena come with the app; any other fight draws its heat on a plain grid
