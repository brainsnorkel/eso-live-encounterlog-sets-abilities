### Added
- **Every death of a fight on its own tab** (#196): the main window's right side is now two tabs on the fight selected in the list, Fight and Deaths. Deaths lists the fight's deaths in the order they came, each with the player, what killed them and from whom, and how long they stayed dead, then the killers with the bosses' share, each player's time dead and the companions' deaths. Click a name for that death on the player's Deaths tab. The header's `Deaths N` opens the tab, Alt+F and Alt+E switch between the two, and Ctrl+PgDn and Ctrl+PgUp step through them from anywhere in the window. The tab you choose stays as you pick other fights and as new ones come in.

### Changed
- **The search sits in the tab bar's corner and searches the tab on screen** (#196): `Find (Ctrl+F)` is at the right of the tabs, so the fight keeps the height the search's own row took, and the count of matches is inside the field. At a large text size in a narrow window it takes a row of its own above the tabs.
