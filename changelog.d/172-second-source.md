### Added
- **A second copy of a group buff set, or of a Champion Point star, that added next to nothing** (#172): two new possible build issues. One flags a player whose copy of a group buff set (Spell Power Cure, Pearlescent Ward, Lucent Echoes, Master Architect, Powerful Assault and the like) the log credited with less than 2% of the other players' time in every fight of a minute or more of the session that another wearer was in, while the other copy did reach the group: a player has such a buff from one wearer at a time. The other flags a second Enlivening Overflow whose procs were under 15% of the holders', or a second From the Brink that never procced while the other did: both stars' cooldown per player is shared by everyone who slots them. The user guide's new *Group effects and a second source* says which group effects a second source can add to and which it cannot, with the numbers behind it
